# Supplier referral agreement: DRAFT for lawyer review

**This is a plain-English starting draft, not legal advice. I am not a lawyer. Have a Florida attorney review it before you ask a yard to sign, especially the fee, no-circumvention, governing-law and dispute clauses.**

Fill the [brackets]. Keep it short on purpose: yards will sign a one-pager faster than a contract.

---

**REFERRAL AGREEMENT**

This agreement is between **[ELX legal name or Callum Weiss, trading as Everglades Lumber Exchange]** of [address] ("ELX") and **[Supplier legal name]** of [address] ("Supplier"), effective [date].

**1. What ELX does.** ELX introduces buyers of building materials to Supplier. ELX does not sell, store or deliver materials and does not handle payment for materials.

**2. Introduced buyer.** A buyer is "introduced" when ELX sends the buyer's request to Supplier, with the buyer's approval, and the request or buyer's identity was not already a customer or open quote of Supplier. If Supplier says it already has the buyer as a customer, it will tell ELX in writing within 3 business days of receiving the request. Otherwise the buyer counts as introduced.

**3. Referral fee.** If an introduced buyer places an order with Supplier, Supplier pays ELX **5%** of the material total of that order (before sales tax, delivery and other fees). The fee applies to the first order and to any further orders by that buyer within **[6 / 12] months** of the introduction.

**4. Reporting and payment.** Supplier will tell ELX about each order from an introduced buyer within **5 business days** of the order, with the order total. ELX will send an invoice and Supplier will pay it within **30 days** of the order date. If an order is later cancelled or materials are returned and refunded, the fee is adjusted to the amount actually kept by Supplier.

**5. Records.** On reasonable request, Supplier will give ELX proof of the order total for an introduced buyer, such as the invoice with the buyer's identity redacted if needed.

**6. Supplier's responsibilities.** Supplier sets its own prices, availability, delivery, tax and payment terms, and is solely responsible to the buyer for the materials and any dispute. ELX makes no promise about the volume of requests, exclusivity or any territory.

**7. Buyer information.** Supplier will use buyer contact details only to quote and fulfil the order, will not sell or share them, and will not use them to avoid the fee in section 3.

**8. Term.** Either party can end this agreement at any time with written notice. Fees for introductions made before the end still apply as stated in section 3.

**9. No other relationship.** The parties are independent. This agreement is not an employment, partnership or agency.

**10. Governing law.** [State of Florida]. [Dispute resolution: attorney to advise.]

Signed:

ELX: ______________________  Name: ____________  Date: ________

Supplier: __________________  Name: ____________  Date: ________

## Notes for you (not part of the agreement)

- **What you currently promised yards:** 5% of order total before tax and delivery, paid within 30 days, and no buyer name until agreed. This draft matches that. The 6/12-month tail and the 5-business-day reporting are **new asks**. Decide whether you want them; they are the usual way referral deals avoid "thanks for the intro, we'll take it from here."
- **Biggest practical risk:** the yard sees the buyer's name and takes future orders directly. Clause 3's tail and clause 7 address this, but a small yard may not agree to them. A 6-month tail is easier to get than 12.
- **Enforcement is hard** for a one-person company outside the US. Be realistic: this works on trust and relationship. Start with yards that reply quickly and seem straightforward.
- **Record every order** in the admin ("Orders and referral fee"), so you know what you are owed and when it is overdue.
- You will likely need a US tax advisor to confirm whether you owe US tax on referral income and how a South African owner should invoice a US company. I haven't verified this.

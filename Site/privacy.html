Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Everglades Lumber Exchange ("ELX", "we", "us", "our") operates evergladeslumber.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our forms:

  • Quote requests: role, ZIP code, project type, urgency, material list, and email address.
  • Supplier applications: company name, contact name, email, phone, location, service radius, categories, website, and additional notes.

We do not automatically collect cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics beyond standard server logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, time of visit) provided by our hosting platform.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill quote requests by matching them with relevant suppliers.
  • To build and maintain our supplier directory.
  • To communicate with you about your request or application.
  • To improve the Site and our matching service.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information sharing

When the structured inbox is configured, quote requests and supplier applications are stored in ELX's Cloudflare D1 database and reviewed by ELX. If that inbox is not configured, the forms use FormSubmit to deliver submissions to ELX. If transactional email is configured, Resend delivers a receipt to the submitting email address and a reference-only notification to ELX. Material details entered into Lumber Scout or the automated first-pass intake review may be sent without contact email, phone, or contact name to Groq when the AI service is configured. Do not enter personal or confidential details into Lumber Scout.

Data retention

We retain form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service. You may request deletion of your data by emailing hello@evergladeslumber.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us at hello@evergladeslumber.com to make a request.

Third-party services

The Site uses Cloudflare Workers for hosting and, when enabled, Cloudflare D1 for the private lead inbox. FormSubmit is used as the form-delivery fallback until D1 intake is enabled. Optional transactional notifications use Resend. When configured, Cloudflare Turnstile processes security-challenge tokens and necessary browser/network signals to distinguish automated form traffic; its widget does not read form entries. When the server-side AI feature is configured, Lumber Scout and intake triage send the role, ZIP code, project description, material list, timing, or supplier product details to Groq for a first-pass review; contact names, emails, and phone numbers are excluded from AI requests. If AI is unavailable, intake triage uses local keyword rules and Scout uses a local keyword review. AI output is a draft and is never a supplier match, quote, technical determination, or publication approval. Each provider processes information under its own terms; ELX does not control its systems.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be noted by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

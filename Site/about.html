About

One person, one job: get you real prices from lumber yards.

I’m Callum Weiss. I run Everglades Lumber Exchange myself.

What ELX is

ELX is a small independent service for people who buy lumber and building materials in South Florida. You send a material list. I ask several yards for current pricing, availability and delivery terms, and send you the comparison. If you pick a yard, you buy directly from it.

What ELX is not

  • Not a lumber yard. I don’t stock, sell or deliver materials.
  • Not affiliated with any yard or supplier, including ones with a similar name.
  • Not a payment processor. The yard invoices you and you pay the yard.

How ELX makes money

Buyers pay nothing. Some yards agree to pay ELX a referral fee, currently 5% of the material total, when they complete an order ELX introduced. Others don’t, and I introduce buyers to them anyway when they’re the best fit. The fee is paid by the yard, never added to your price.

How your information is handled

Your contact details are shared with a yard only after you approve the match. See the privacy policy for details.

Why I started this

Lumber prices and stock change from yard to yard and week to week, and getting several current quotes means a lot of calls. ELX is a way to ask once and compare. It is new, so the supplier network is still growing and I’m building it one yard at a time.

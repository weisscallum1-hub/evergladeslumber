FAQ

Designed to launch lean.

Is this the same as the old Everglades Lumber?

No. The business previously known as Everglades Lumber has permanently closed. Everglades Lumber Exchange (ELX) is a new, independently operated materials sourcing marketplace with no affiliation to any prior business operating under a similar name.

Why not sell or dropship lumber directly?

Large-format construction materials carry freight complexity, local-stock volatility and significant damage risk in transit. The first version monetizes the lead and routing layer instead. Commerce can be layered on for smaller, higher-margin products once demand is proven.

What does Lumber Scout actually do?

Lumber Scout normalizes a plain-English material request into a structured, supplier-ready RFQ. It groups materials into product families, flags missing dimensions or quantities, captures ZIP and urgency, and passes a clean request to matched local suppliers.

How do suppliers get matched to my request?

The matching layer uses territory, material categories, delivery radius, minimum order and response SLA to route each request to the right supplier set. As the supplier registry grows, the matches get tighter and faster.

Can this expand beyond South Florida?

Yes. The Everglades positioning gives the launch a geographic story, but the software layer can support additional metro directories over time. South Florida is the density-first market.

What does it cost buyers to use ELX?

Nothing. Buyers submit quote requests for free. The marketplace is supplier-funded through lead fees and directory placements.

South Florida supply market

What contractors buy in Miami-Dade & Broward

🪵 Framing & Structural Lumber — Miami

Miami-Dade contractors source framing lumber locally due to hurricane-grade requirements and permitting standards. Common requests:

  • 2×4, 2×6, 2×8 SPF #2 and SYP
  • Pressure-treated posts & plates
  • LVL beams and engineered lumber
  • Hurricane-rated connectors & hardware

🏗️ Plywood & Panels — Broward

Broward County builds require impact-rated sheathing and specific panel grades for wind uplift compliance. Common requests:

  • 7/16 and 1/2 OSB sheathing
  • CDX plywood for roof decking
  • Marine-grade for outdoor applications
  • Cabinet-grade birch and maple

🌴 Decking & Outdoor — South Florida

Florida's humidity and saltwater exposure demand material specs beyond standard decking. Common requests:

  • Composite decking (Trex, TimberTech)
  • Pressure-treated ACQ for ground contact
  • Ipe and tropical hardwood
  • Aluminum rail systems

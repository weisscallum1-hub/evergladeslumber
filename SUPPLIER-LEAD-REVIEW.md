## Supplier quote checklist

If the request is authenticated, get supplier quotes directly and compare like-for-like:

1. Confirm intended application and whether the required panel is standard CDX Exposure 1 or another specified performance category; request grade/APA stamp, veneer species, ply count, edge, span rating and any project-required certification. Do not silently substitute a different plywood product for an outdoor/deck application.
2. Ask for quantity in full units/packs and sheets, current stock versus mill order, earliest ready date, split-load options and damage/shortage claims process.
3. Request per-sheet and extended price, named FOB origin point, separately stated delivery to the exact Redwood City jobsite, unloading requirements, taxes/fees, quote expiration and any price escalation conditions.
4. Ask for the supplier's own payment and credit-approval process. Do not promise the buyer Net 10–20 terms before that supplier approves them in writing.
5. Obtain the buyer's independently verified company/project details and approval before disclosing their contact details or forwarding their RFQ to suppliers.

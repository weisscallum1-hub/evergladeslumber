# Buyer outreach and testimonial templates

Fill the [brackets]. Keep every claim true: ELX is new, you have a small number of yards, and quotes come from yards.

## Before you send anything

- **CAN-SPAM (US law for commercial email):** every message needs your real name, an honest subject line, a **valid physical mailing address** and a working way to opt out. You are outside the US, so decide which address you will print (a registered agent or mailbox service address works) and add it to the footer line below.
- Send from hello@evergladeslumber.com, one person at a time, to real businesses with a clear reason to buy lumber. No bought lists, no mass blasts. 20 to 30 well-chosen emails a week is plenty.
- Find targets by searching Google Maps and Google for [sign companies / fence contractors / deck builders / remodelers / property managers] in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach, then use the contact email on their own website.
- Only mention what you can deliver. Do not say "we have X yards" until it is true.

## Testimonial request (send only after a buyer has actually ordered and is happy)

**Subject:** One small favour

Hi [first name],

Glad the [materials] order worked out. If you're happy to, could you reply with one or two sentences on how it went? For example, was the comparison useful and did the order arrive as expected?

I'd like to put it on the site with your first name and company. If you would rather I leave your name off, say so and I will. If it didn't go as well as you hoped, I'd like to hear that too.

Thanks,
Callum

**Rules for using it:**
- Use the buyer's words exactly. Do not edit or invent quotes.
- Get written permission (their reply is enough) for the name and company.
- Do not publish anything until a real order has happened. A page with no testimonials is better than a made-up one.

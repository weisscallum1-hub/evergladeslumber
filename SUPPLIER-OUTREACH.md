## Outreach rules

- Contact only through the supplier’s published business channel; identify ELX as independent.
- Do not describe a supplier as a partner until it has explicitly opted in and ELX has independently verified the business and listing details.
- Do not send a buyer’s name, email, project, or material list during directory recruitment.
- Do not promise lead volume, exclusive territories, prices, availability, credit, or payment terms.
- Record the date, channel, recipient role, reply, profile permission, and verification evidence in the ELX lead inbox after the inbox is configured.

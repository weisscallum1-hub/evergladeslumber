Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Everglades Lumber Exchange ("ELX", "we", "us", "our") operates evergladeslumber.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our forms:

  • Quote requests: role, ZIP code, project type, urgency, material list, and email address.
  • Supplier applications: company name, contact name, email, phone, location, service radius, categories, website, and additional notes.

We do not automatically collect cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics beyond standard server logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, time of visit) provided by our hosting platform.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill quote requests by matching them with relevant suppliers.
  • To build and maintain our supplier directory.
  • To communicate with you about your request or application.
  • To improve the Site and our matching service.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information sharing

Buyer contact information is shared only with suppliers matched to a specific quote request, and only with your implicit consent by submitting a request. Supplier information submitted for directory listing may be published publicly on the Site.

Data retention

We retain form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service. You may request deletion of your data by emailing hello@evergladeslumber.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us at hello@evergladeslumber.com to make a request.

Third-party services

The Site uses FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) for form processing. Form submissions are handled in accordance with FormSubmit's own privacy policy. The Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; standard Cloudflare infrastructure logs apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be noted by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
